Please keep in mind that this app is still young and being tested. Before you make a real Namkha, have the results checked by someone experienced in these calculations.

A Namkha is a shamanic astrological thread-cross that comes from Bön, the pre-Buddhist tradition of Tibet. Thread-crosses serve a range of purposes, among them harmonization and protection. Chögyal Namkhai Norbu rediscovered a method for calculating and constructing such thread-crosses for the benefit of all sentient beings. Their activity rests on the principles of Tibetan astrology, and they are empowered with a corresponding ritual.

The Namkha Calculator performs four types of personal Namkha calculation, one for each astrological period of a birth:

  • for the Tibetan hour of birth
  • for the Tibetan day of birth
  • for the Tibetan month of birth
  • for the Tibetan year of birth

The year and the month Namkha are available now; the day and the hour Namkha are coming soon.

This is the first program to calculate these types of Namkha, which are described in Namkha: Harmonizing Energy of the Elements by Chögyal Namkhai Norbu. It brings the full potential of the method, and supports practitioners and everyone else with an interest in it.

The program was created by two people: Anna Weiss, a Namkha expert and Tibetan astrologer who has worked directly with Shang Shung Publications Italy, and Simon Weiss, who is responsible for the technical side of it.

It takes account of the developments that followed the publication of the latest edition of Namkha: Harmonizing Energy of the Elements in 2022:

  • separate calculations for women and for men
  • four types of Namkha, one for each of the four astrological periods of birth

The program is based on:

  • Namkha: Harmonizing Energy of the Elements by Chögyal Namkhai Norbu (Shang Shung Publications, 2022)
  • Jung-We Khyil-Khor: Mandala of the Five Elements by Migmar Tsering (2020)
  • Tibetan Calendar Mathematics by Svante Janson (2014)

How to use the Namkha Calculator

Fill in the date, time, and place of birth, then press the Calculate button (if you are unsure of the birth time, enter an approximate time or simply noon). The app works out the harmonization of the elements and prepares a calculation sheet for you: a color table for every part of the Namkha, notes worth knowing about the calculation, and a picture of how the finished Namkha should look. You can read the sheet right here on the page, or download it as a PDF file that is ready to print.

You do not need to know the time zone of the birth – the app finds it on its own from the place and the date, and it knows the historical time rules, so births from long ago are handled too. If you do know the time zone, you can of course set it yourself.

The app is free to use. Its source code is public as well, so anyone can improve it or build on it. It is made with care by Namkha Encyclopedia.
