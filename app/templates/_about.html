About

A Namkha is a traditional Tibetan thread-cross: coloured threads wound around wooden sticks in a pattern that is individual to each person, based on Tibetan astrology. This app calculates the colours for a Namkha in the tradition of Chögyal Namkhai Norbu Rinpoche.

Fill in the date, time, and place of birth, then press the Calculate button (if you are unsure of the birth time, enter an approximate time or simply noon). The app works out the harmonization of the elements and prepares a calculation sheet for you: a colour table for every part of the Namkha, notes worth knowing about the calculation, and a picture of how the finished Namkha should look. You can read the sheet right here on the page, or download it as a PDF file that is ready to print.

You do not need to know the time zone of the birth – the app finds it on its own from the place and the date, and it knows the historical time rules, so births from long ago are handled too. If you do know the time zone, you can of course set it yourself.

Please keep in mind that this app is still young and being tested. Before you make a real Namkha, have the results checked by someone experienced in these calculations.

The app is free to use. It is made with care by Namkha Encyclopedia.
