About

This web app calculates a Namkha thread-cross colour scheme in the tradition of Chögyal Namkhai Norbu Rinpoche. Enter the date, time, and place of birth, and it computes the harmonization of the elements and renders a printable sheet with the colour table and an illustration of the made Namkha.

The project is in an alpha stage – all calculations should be checked manually when making a real Namkha.
