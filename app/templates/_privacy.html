Privacy policy

To calculate a Namkha, the app needs the details you enter in the form: the name (optional), gender, and the date, time, and place of birth. They are sent to our server, used for the calculation, and shown on your sheet. Nothing else is collected.

We keep a technical log of each calculation – the birth details you entered and the calculated result – so that we can find and fix mistakes. The name is treated differently: it is never written to the log as text, only as a short scrambled code that cannot be turned back into the name.

When you enter a birth place, the text you enter is sent to Photon, an outside service that provides the place suggestions. It only receives what you enter in that one field.

To keep automated abuse from overloading the calculator, each calculation is checked by Cloudflare Turnstile, a service that tells people and bots apart. It runs in your browser, receives your IP address and technical details about your browser, and hands us a one-time ticket that our server checks with Cloudflare. Most of the time you will not notice it; if it is unsure, it shows a check box above the Calculate button. It is not advertising and does not follow you across other sites.

There are no user accounts, no advertising, and no tracking or analytics services on this site. Apart from what the bot check above needs to work, we set no cookies. We do not sell or share your details with anyone.
