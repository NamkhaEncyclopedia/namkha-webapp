Privacy policy

To calculate a Namkha, the app needs the details you enter in the form: the name (optional), gender, and the date, time, and place of birth. They are sent to our server, used for the calculation, and shown on your sheet. Nothing else is collected.

We keep a technical log of each calculation – the birth details you entered and the calculated result – so that we can find and fix mistakes. The name is treated differently: it is never written to the log as text, only as a short scrambled code that cannot be turned back into the name.

When you type in the birth place field, the text you type is sent to Photon, an outside service that provides the place suggestions. It only receives what you type in that one field.

There are no user accounts, no cookies, no advertising, and no tracking or analytics services on this site. We do not sell or share your details with anyone.
